# Arte de Arcade / Arcade artwork

## Español

Los interiores conservan sus distribuciones, objetivos y colisiones. Comparten iluminación nocturna, reflejos, materiales y mobiliario isométrico con la ciudad exterior. Seis personajes secundarios nuevos siguen las proporciones y el detalle del protagonista. Los recursos se cargan antes de preparar las texturas de Godot; la caché se actualiza cuando termina una descarga.

Generación: herramienta integrada ImageGen, con `antonio-atlas.webp` y `company-district.webp` como referencias de estilo. Se pidió un fondo de croma uniforme para preparar recortes transparentes; se separaron las celdas, eliminaron los píxeles del croma y exportaron a WebP. El atlas inicial de personajes mostró un fondo de tablero, así que una segunda generación lo sustituyó por croma antes de preparar los recursos. No se modificaron las referencias originales.

Recursos finales en `public/images/job-route/`: `npc-engineer.webp`, `npc-doctor.webp`, `npc-musician.webp`, `npc-dancer.webp`, `npc-child.webp`, `npc-family.webp` y `furniture-{desk,table,sofa,bed,plant,rack,board,coffee,washer,sink,router,phone-blue,phone-green,skeleton,bookcase,lamp}.webp`. Librería y lámpara quedan disponibles para futuras distribuciones; no añaden obstáculos a partidas existentes.

Especificación de personajes: seis figuras aisladas en cuadrícula de tres columnas por dos filas, cuerpos completos y vista de tres cuartos, con contornos oscuros, pliegues, rostros y cabello detallados, luz cian y violeta. Ingeniera con chaqueta turquesa; médico con bata; músico con saxofón; bailarina con chándal burdeos; niña con dibujo; mujer con jersey coral. Referencia: protagonista del juego.

Especificación de mobiliario: dieciséis piezas aisladas en cuadrícula de cuatro por cuatro, proyección isométrica 2:1 y materiales densamente detallados como el exterior; sombras azul marino, lámparas ámbar y bordes turquesa. Puesto informático, mesa de comedor, sofá, cama, planta, rack, panel, cafetera, lavadora, fregadero, router, dos teléfonos monumentales, esqueleto, librería y lámpara. Fondo verde uniforme reservado al croma, sin textos ni suelos incrustados. Los prompts originales exactos se conservan debajo como datos de producción.

La música está activada por defecto; se respetan las preferencias explícitas de silencio. La campaña conserva su guardado local. Misión rápida permite elegir cualquier reto, usa una partida independiente y conserva su estado al cambiar de idioma en la misma pestaña.

## English

Interiors preserve their layouts, objectives and collisions. They share night lighting, reflections, materials and isometric furniture with the exterior town. Six new NPCs follow the protagonist's proportions and detail. Assets load before Godot textures are prepared; the cache refreshes when a download completes.

Generation: built-in ImageGen, using `antonio-atlas.webp` and `company-district.webp` as style references. A uniform chroma-key background was requested for transparent cutouts; cells were separated, chroma pixels removed and assets exported to WebP. The initial NPC atlas included a checkerboard background, so a second generation replaced it with chroma before asset preparation. Original references were preserved.

Final assets in `public/images/job-route/`: `npc-engineer.webp`, `npc-doctor.webp`, `npc-musician.webp`, `npc-dancer.webp`, `npc-child.webp`, `npc-family.webp` and `furniture-{desk,table,sofa,bed,plant,rack,board,coffee,washer,sink,router,phone-blue,phone-green,skeleton,bookcase,lamp}.webp`. The bookcase and lamp are available for future layouts; they add no obstacles to existing saves.

NPC specification: six isolated full-body figures in a three-column, two-row grid, three-quarter view, dark outlines, detailed fabric folds, faces and hair, cyan and violet rim lighting. Engineer with teal jacket; doctor in a lab coat; saxophone musician; dancer in burgundy sportswear; little girl holding a drawing; woman wearing a coral sweater. Reference: the playable protagonist.

Furniture specification: sixteen isolated pieces in a four-by-four grid, 2:1 isometric projection and the exterior's densely detailed materials; navy shadows, amber lamps and teal edges. Developer workstation, dining table, sofa, bed, plant, rack, board, coffee machine, washer, sink, router, two phone monuments, skeleton, bookcase and lamp. Uniform green background reserved for chroma key, no baked-in labels or floors. Exact original prompts are retained below as production data.

Music is enabled by default, respecting explicit mute preferences. Campaign retains its local save. Quick mission allows any challenge, uses an independent run and retains its state when switching language in the same tab.

## Prompts originales / Original prompts

### Personajes / Characters

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: production game NPC sprite atlas with genuine transparent alpha background. Input image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match the supplied playable character's polished detailed pixel-art RPG style, body/head proportions, black pixel outlines, fabric folds, rich shading, cyan left rim light and subtle violet right rim light. Create SIX DIFFERENT NPC characters, not Antonio clones. Exact layout: a 3 column by 2 row regular grid, each of the six equally sized cells contains ONE full-body character centered with generous transparent margins and no overlap. All stand in a front three-quarter view facing slightly right, feet aligned near bottom of their cell. Top row: (1) female software engineer with short dark hair, teal jacket, dark jeans, glasses, badge; (2) adult male doctor in white lab coat over dark teal scrubs, stethoscope and coffee mug; (3) balding male musician with round glasses, ochre cardigan, saxophone held in front. Bottom row: (4) female dancer with tied-up hair, burgundy tracksuit, one arm raised; (5) little girl with chestnut ponytail and yellow cardigan holding a sheet of drawing paper, child proportions; (6) adult woman with chestnut bob haircut, coral sweater and navy trousers. Each character has detailed face, individual eyes, hair strands, clothing seams, pockets and shaded shoes. Consistent scale for all adults. Child smaller. Crisp richly shaded pixel illustration as detailed as the reference. No text, no labels, no boxes, no scenery, no contact sheet borders, no checkerboard baked into background. Only six isolated transparent sprites.

### Corrección del fondo / Background correction

Precise background edit of this production sprite sheet. Keep ALL SIX characters exactly as drawn, same grid arrangement and pixel details and proportions. Replace ALL of the checkerboard background and its noisy shapes with ONE PURE FLAT SOLID #00FF00 chroma-key green color, perfectly uniform RGB 0,255,0 everywhere outside characters including gaps between limbs. No gradients, no texture, no checkerboard, no shadows on green. Do not recolor any character. This is for lossless chroma-key extraction into individual transparent game sprites. Same 1536x1024 canvas.

### Mobiliario / Furniture

Use case: stylized-concept. Production isometric furniture sprite atlas for the INTERIORS of the supplied reference town, which is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Match its exquisite densely detailed illustrated pixel art, dark navy shadows, warm amber lamps, teal rim lighting, rich material textures, same 2:1 isometric projection with vertical upright edges. Output a 4 COLUMN x 4 ROW grid of 16 equally sized square cells on a PERFECTLY UNIFORM PURE SOLID RGB (0,255,0) #00FF00 chroma-key background. No text or labels. Each cell contains exactly one isolated complete furnishing, fully within cell with generous green margin, consistent viewing angle with the front of the item facing down-left. No floor tiles, no backdrops, no rooms. Furniture fills 75% of cell. Top row from left: 1 wooden developer workstation with detailed monitor showing tiny colorful code, keyboard, mouse, notebook, mug, under-desk cables and small office chair tucked under it; 2 wooden dining table with dishes, fruit bowl and papers; 3 cozy blue-gray upholstered sofa with amber and burgundy cushions and folded throw; 4 wooden single bed with midnight blue quilt, pillow and rumpled sheets. Row 2: 5 luxuriant potted indoor plant with many crisp individual leaves in terracotta planter (NO PURE GREEN on plant: only desaturated dark teal foliage); 6 dark server rack with glass door, shelves, cables, cyan and amber status LEDs; 7 freestanding technical planning whiteboard on wheels with notes, colorful sticky notes and small flowchart, no legible text; 8 detailed espresso machine and ceramic mug on a kitchen base cabinet. Row 3: 9 white front-loading washing machine with round glass drum and controls; 10 kitchen sink cabinet with silver faucet, dishes and sponge; 11 small dark WiFi router with antennas and amber LEDs sitting on small wooden side table; 12 large blue Nokia-3310-like retro mobile phone monument on small stone plinth, screen and keypad but NO text. Row 4: 13 large muted sage-green old mobile phone monument on stone plinth with amber fairground light bulbs, green handset must be muted gray-green NOT chroma-key green; 14 ivory human skeleton teaching prop on metal standing support; 15 tall walnut bookcase with many richly textured colorful books and objects; 16 elegant warm glowing floor lamp with brass base and cream shade. No people except the skeleton prop. Polished densely shaded illustration, not flat vector, not crude blocks, no checkerboard. Solid #00FF00 background reserved for keying, not used on objects. Objects separate, do not touch.
