This is a development log for the first foundation of ANTONIOMDM OS.

## One portfolio, two ways to explore

The desktop is the entry point. Projects, experience and notes have their own URLs and render as HTML. Interactive windows add another way to explore that same content.

## A small boundary for Arcade

Arcade starts with a separate entry screen. Its module loads only after choosing **Enter**. A future world can use the same project and career data without keeping a second content catalogue.
